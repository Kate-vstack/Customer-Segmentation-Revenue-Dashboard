# Customer-Segmentation-Revenue-Dashboard
Customer Segmentation and Revenue Dashboard Using Power BI | Excel | DAX | Data Modelling

**PROJECT TITLE** :Customer Segmentation and Revenue Analysis.

**BUSINESS PROBLEM** :
WirelessNG which is a leading Unlimited Internet Service Provider(ISP) lacked visibility into subscriber behavior, plan performance, and revenue drivers making it difficult to make confident, data backed decisions.I built an analytics dashboard to surface trends in customer acquisition, engagement, retention, and revenue, with findings translated into concrete recommendations to support growth and profitability.

**TOOL USED** :
1) Microsoft Power BI(Dasboard development)
2) Microsoft Excel (Data cleaning)
3) SQL(Quering)
4) Python(Analysis)


**KPIs**
1) Total Customers
2) Revenue Generated
3) Growth Rate
4) Peak Acquisition year
   
**KEY INSIGHT**
1) Regular Plans Generated Over 91% of the total revenue
2) Customer Acquisition peaked in 2023 before declining gradually.
3) Revnue performance was higher on regular plan than night plan
4) Unlimited Business-Pro plan generated more revenue therefore showing revenue efficiency despite lower customer volume.

**RECOMMENDATION**:
1) Increase investment in unlimited business subscription plan by giving discounts to attract more customers to the plan. 
2) Introduce retention campaign for inactive customers.
3) Improve upselling strategies targeting lower tier subscribers.
4) Carry out more campaign and promotional offers on high-perfomrning plans.
